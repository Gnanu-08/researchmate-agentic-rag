ACADEMIC RULES — DEMO INSTITUTION

Attendance:
Students are expected to maintain at least 75 percent attendance in each registered course unless an approved institutional exception applies.

Assessment:
Courses may contain internal assessments, assignments, laboratory work, and final examinations. Exact weightage is specified in the course syllabus.

Academic integrity:
Submitted academic work must be the student's own work unless collaboration is explicitly permitted. Plagiarism and unauthorized assistance may be handled under institutional disciplinary procedures.

Leave:
Students should follow the institution's prescribed leave process and retain supporting documentation where required.
