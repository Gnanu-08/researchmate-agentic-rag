PLACEMENT GUIDELINES — DEMO INSTITUTION

Registration:
Eligible final-year students may register with the placement cell by submitting the required registration form and current resume.

Typical process:
The placement process may include resume screening, an aptitude assessment, a technical interview, a managerial or HR interview, and final selection. The exact process varies by employer.

Documents:
Students should keep a resume, student identity card, academic records, and any employer-specific documents ready.

Eligibility:
Employers may set academic percentage, backlog, branch, graduation-year, or other eligibility conditions. Students should verify the conditions in the specific employer notice.

Conduct:
Students who register for an employer process are expected to follow placement-cell instructions and attend scheduled stages.
