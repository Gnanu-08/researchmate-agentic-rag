INTERNSHIP POLICY — DEMO INSTITUTION

Eligibility:
Students who have completed at least two academic semesters may apply for an internship. Applicants must have an active student status during the application period.

Required documents:
Applicants must submit a current resume, student identity card, academic transcript, and an internship application form. If the host organization requires additional documents, the placement office may request them.

Selection:
The host organization may conduct interviews or screening tests. Final selection is made by the host organization.

Internship duration:
The standard internship duration is between six and twelve weeks. Any extension requires approval from the academic coordinator.

Academic requirements:
Students must submit an internship completion certificate and a short report after completion.
